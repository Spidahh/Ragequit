# Vision & Pitch

## What RAGEQUIT is

A PvP 3D browser arena combat game. Skill-based, build-crafting at its core,
with active arena-FPS aim and movement: weapons, spells, aerial responses,
movement tech and class-specific builds must create a fast duel where the player
is always doing something meaningful.

## What makes it different

1. **Classes create real play identities**. Tank, Arciere, Mago and Ibrido
   change slot distribution, resource emphasis, weapon access and class mechanic.
2. **Build-crafting IS the gameplay**. The chosen class defines the build grammar;
   the selected abilities decide counters and combo routes.
3. **Movement is combat**. Falling never hurts, own spells never self-damage,
   all weapon families can matter in air, and spell/weapon impulses may become
   deliberate movement tech.
4. **Air combos stay interactive**. Knockups and air punish windows are
   important, but the victim must retain skill-based answers through aim,
   allowed actions, movement abilities and disruption.
5. **Fair elements**. No rock-paper-scissors between elements. Each element has a distinct effect (burn / slow / chain / lifesteal / DoT) — none is strong or weak against another. Your build choices aren't punished at loadout lock by the enemy's matchup.
6. **Long TTK (20-30s)**. Fights are duels of positioning, resource management, and combo execution — not one-shots.
7. **Server-authoritative zero-trust**. No auto-aim, no client trust, no RNG in ability output. Pure meritocracy.
8. **No paywalls**. No season pass, no loot boxes. Everything earned through play and quests.

## Game Feel

- **Fluid**: arena-FPS pace with crosshair-first aim, weapon/spell transitions,
  active air combat and no artificial lockout that turns a launch into a
  spectator moment.
- **Readable**: stylized VFX with element-coded colors (red / cyan / yellow / purple / green). Silhouette-first visuals.
- **Punishing but fair**: knockup enables combos, but parry and positioning defeat it. Misreads cost, but they never snowball from gear.
- **Replayable**: deep build space, quest-based permanent ability unlocks, ELO ranked ladder across 7 tiers.
