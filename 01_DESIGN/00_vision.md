# Vision & Pitch

## What RAGEQUIT is

A PvP 3D browser arena combat game. Skill-based, build-crafting at its core, fluid combat with three weapon modes (Sword / Bow / Staff) and five magic elements fully intertwined through the Element Mastery system.

## What makes it different

1. **All weapons always accessible**. Every player wields sword, bow, and staff. No class ever locks a weapon away.
2. **Build-crafting IS the gameplay**. Your 7-ability loadout defines your identity. You combine for synergies or spread for flexibility — both are valid strategies.
3. **Element Mastery infuses basic attacks**. Stack 4+ magic abilities of the same element and your Mastery activates: you get the element's bonus AND your current weapon's M1 is infused with that element (Fire archer shoots flaming arrows; Ice knight swings slowing strikes).
4. **Knockup-based combat as signature**. Abilities tagged [KNOCKUP] send enemies airborne and disable their abilities in the air, opening combo windows.
5. **Fair elements**. No rock-paper-scissors between elements. Each element has a distinct effect (burn / slow / chain / lifesteal / DoT) — none is strong or weak against another. Your build choices aren't punished at loadout lock by the enemy's matchup.
6. **Long TTK (20-30s)**. Fights are duels of positioning, resource management, and combo execution — not one-shots.
7. **Server-authoritative zero-trust**. No auto-aim, no client trust, no RNG in ability output. Pure meritocracy.
8. **No paywalls**. No season pass, no loot boxes. Everything earned through play and quests.

## Target feel

- **Fluid**: instant weapon swap via Tab/scroll, with Q reserved for the Utility Wheel and E reserved for the Ability Wheel. No "drawing animation" lockouts.
- **Readable**: stylized VFX with element-coded colors (red / cyan / yellow / purple / green). Silhouette-first visuals.
- **Punishing but fair**: knockup enables combos, but parry and positioning defeat it. Misreads cost, but they never snowball from gear.
- **Replayable**: deep build space, quest-based permanent ability unlocks, ELO ranked ladder across 7 tiers.
